Tier 2 Debug the Hunt Scenario

Read each field scenario. Identify the strategy error and write a correct approach.

Debug #1 — Phase Transition Failure
Scenario: A hunter keeps pointing their Yagi toward the strongest signal direction. After 20 minutes they've walked in a large arc and haven't found any fox. They're confused about why. What phase mistake are they making? What should they be doing differently at close range?
Debug #2 — Signal Disappears
Scenario: A team is tracking a fox when the signal suddenly disappears completely. They assume they are completely lost and sit down to wait. List at least 3 possible technical or procedural reasons why the signal disappeared, and a fix for each.
Debug #3 — Scoring Calculation
Scenario: Hunt starts at 09:00. Time limit 80 minutes = deadline 10:20.
The team logged their foxes as follows:
• MO5 found at 10:00 (60 min elapsed)
• MOE found at 10:10 (70 min elapsed)
• Checked in at Finish at 10:20 (80 min elapsed — exactly on time)
Using the formula score = 80 − elapsed_minutes per fox, calculate their total score. Show your working.
Tier 3 Phase Decision Trainer

In ARDF you switch technique depending on signal strength and distance. Read each scenario and choose the correct hunt phase.

Quick reference:  Phase 1 = Yagi + compass bearing (long range)  ·  Phase 2 = Body shielding (medium, S9++)  ·  Phase 3 = Rubber duck, very close (<30 m)
