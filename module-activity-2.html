Tier 2 — Debug the Scenario Applied

Read each scenario, identify the problem, and explain how to fix it.

📋 Scenario 1: You took two identical bearings of 045° from the same location. Why can't you triangulate? Fix the method.
📋 Scenario 2: Your map shows scale 1:25 000. You measured 4 cm on the map. How far is that in real life? Show your working.
📋 Scenario 3: You recorded a bearing of 450°. What's wrong? Give the correct value.
Scenario 1: Two bearings from the same location cannot triangulate — they are the same line. Fix: move to a second position at least 50 m away before taking the second bearing. Lines from different positions are needed to intersect.
Scenario 2: Scale 1:25 000 means 1 cm = 250 m. Working: 4 cm × 250 m = 1,000 m = 1 km.
Scenario 3: Bearings must be 0°–359°. 450° is invalid because the compass only goes to 360°. Fix: 450° − 360° = 090° (East).

When three bearing lines are used, they rarely meet at a single point — they form a small triangle called a "cocked hat". This is normal and is caused by small measurement errors.
