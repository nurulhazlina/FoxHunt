How Direction Finding Works

A hidden transmitter (the "fox") broadcasts a short Morse identifier every 12 seconds. You rotate your Yagi antenna until you find the direction of maximum signal — that's your bearing.

From two or more positions, your bearing lines intersect at the fox's location. The closer you get, the more you attenuate the signal to avoid overload.
