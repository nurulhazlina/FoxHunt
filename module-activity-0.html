Tier 2 Debug the Scenario

Read each flawed scenario. Identify the mistake and write a clear fix.

Debug #1 — Wrong Navigation Tool
Scenario: A team brought a mobile phone GPS instead of a compass and topographic map. They say it's better because it shows their exact location. What's wrong with this approach? What should they use and why?
Debug #2 — Wrong Radio Band
Scenario: A team is using a 27 MHz CB radio to hunt 144 MHz ARDF beacons. They hear nothing but static. What is the fundamental mistake? What equipment do they actually need?
Debug #3 — Time Penalty Calculation
Scenario: The hunt starts at 09:00 and lasts 80 minutes. A team found Fox 1 at 09:25, Fox 3 at 09:50, and Fox 5 at 10:05. They returned to Finish at 10:35 (95 minutes elapsed). The time limit is 80 minutes. Did they qualify? How is the late return penalised? Calculate their adjusted score.

🦊 Extension Challenge

Add a "find log" feature to the registration system. When a scout finds a fox, they should be able to log:

  • The beacon ID (e.g. MOE, MOI…)
